Smart Touch Switches provide a modern and convenient way to control home appliances.

- Integrated sensor strip 
- Control compatible appliances through a smart home app 
- Real-time power consumption monitoring 
- Convenient control of multiple home appliances 
 
 Appliances You Can Control 
- LED Lights 
- Tube Lights 
- Bulbs 
- Sockets 
- Fans
